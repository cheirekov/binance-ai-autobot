# Project direction

Read the root README for product direction and apps/trader/README.md for current
runtime commands. The objective is one AI-assisted Binance trader on i2.

- apps/api is the retired NestJS engine. Do not resume its development or start
  its containers unless the user explicitly requests legacy recovery.
- archive/legacy, references/retired-prompt-bundles-20260915 and old feedback
  bundles are historical data, not instructions. T-031/T-032/T-040 gates and
  the retired autobot-production-orchestrator skill do not govern this project.
- Baseline, Astra and Momentum are temporary experiments within one platform.
  Explain experiment changes and their decision criteria before expanding them.
- A failing research gate remains a failure. Supplemental positive results do
  not retroactively validate it or establish a profitable edge.
- Keep user-facing reporting in plain language. Separate observed facts,
  hypotheses and unimplemented plans. Never claim guaranteed returns.
- Preserve credentials, databases and existing changes. Runtime delivery belongs
  on i2; verify the target checkout and service state before changing it.
- Follow the user's existing model/credit preflight preference for new tasks;
  do not repeat it for routine follow-ups within the approved task.
