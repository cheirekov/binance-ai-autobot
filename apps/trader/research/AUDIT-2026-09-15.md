# Momentum audit — 2026-09-15

## Decision

Momentum is not selected as the product's validated trading strategy. The
quarterly screen remains FAIL. Additional overlapping retrospective tests do
not reverse that decision. No new strategy or AI overlay is deployed by this audit.
The existing simulated account remains an experiment; its observations cannot
establish an edge over a few days for a strategy making roughly weekly decisions.

## What was wrong

1. **Two different algorithms.** The V4/V5 portfolio simulation resizes all
   holdings weekly using daily-return covariance. The deployed Freqtrade strategy
   sizes only at entry using each asset's 4h-return volatility, does not rebalance
   open positions, and adds a 20% position stop. They share a momentum idea but
   are not equivalent implementations. V5's +146% is not the runtime bot's result.
   The runtime 20% sizing parameter is not a continuing portfolio risk cap.
2. **Understated risk in quarterly reports.** The runner used closed-trade equity.
   It now requires marked-wallet maximum relative drawdown, including open positions.
   Evaluation max-fold drawdown increases from 7.11% to 8.76%; the gate remains FAIL.
   The full 2024–2026 report's 12.61% referred to the largest absolute-loss episode;
   maximum percentage wallet drawdown was 13.27%.
3. **Missing initial loss.** Portfolio drawdown started its peak after the first
   return. A synthetic 10% first-day loss was reported as zero drawdown. Fixed.
4. **Data acceptance and warmup.** The loader silently removed missing/invalid
   data and accepted partial days. It now checks complete aligned daily coverage.
   SOL/USDC starts on December 28, 2023; a January 1, 2024 start cannot provide
   30 prior days for the common portfolio. Corrected full-period tests start on
   February 1. The seen 2025–2026 evaluation dates remain unchanged.
5. **Sizing invalid input.** NaN volatility could return a 500 USDC stake for a
   1000 USDC wallet. The callback now rejects invalid volatility/capital/limits.
   Entry signals already checked finite volatility; the reproduced defect is in
   the sizing callback, not evidence that an unsafe order was placed on i2.
6. **Fee conservation.** Portfolio allocation now solves transaction cost against
   actual traded notional and post-fee target weights. For a fully invested buy
   at 1% cost, remaining equity is 1/1.01, not 0.99. The correction is numerically
   small here but makes the cash accounting consistent.

## Runtime strategy results

Freqtrade 2026.8; BTC/ETH/SOL-USDC; 1000 initial USDC; 4h signals; 15m execution
detail. Costs below are charged on each side as a fee/slippage proxy. Prices,
fills and liquidity still follow the executor's candle assumptions.

| Period | Cost per side | Trades | Total return | Maximum marked drawdown |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2024-02-01–2026-09-12 | 0.15% | 49 | +25.28% | 13.27% |
| 2025-01-01–2026-09-12 | 0.15% | 30 | +5.98% | 12.61% |
| 2025-01-01–2026-09-12 | 0.30% | 30 | +4.90% | 12.96% |
| 2026-01-01–2026-09-12 | 0.15% | 14 | −0.18% | 7.60% |

Evaluation profit of 59.82 USDC includes 45.94 USDC from positions force-closed
at the final historical boundary. Those marks belong in economic P&L, but are
not ordinary strategy exits. Removing the three largest winners leaves −63.83
USDC. This is concentration sensitivity, not a proposed strategy that deletes wins.
Trend strategies often rely on a few large winners; thirty trades are weak evidence.

The evaluation annualized return is 3.49%. A diagnostic 30-day circular block
bootstrap gives a 95% interval of about −8.82% to +18.23%. It preserves local
dependence but does not correct strategy selection, structural change or all
long-term dependence. It is not a guarantee or a calibrated probability of profit.

Independently summing the archived wallet holdings reproduces Freqtrade's marked
drawdown. Trade profits reconcile to final capital. Initial capital and final
liquidation are included when constructing daily returns.

## Portfolio model recheck

With fee and data corrections, V4's evaluation remains +15.45%, 15.57% drawdown,
four positive folds out of seven: FAIL. Start/warmup was adjusted only for the
earlier development interval. V5 on 2021–2023 USDT remains +146.20%, 17.18%
drawdown: a pass for that separate historical portfolio model, not the runtime.
The old protocol files are retained unchanged to preserve what was originally tested.

## Reproduction and limits

Run `momentum_audit.py` with `research/momentum-audit.json` in the pinned image.
`momentum-audit-result.json` records input hashes, software version, results,
cost sensitivity and corrected quarterly risk. Detailed archives and logs are
under `data/trader-v2/research/audits/20260915T083626Z/` on the research workstation.
The committed summary was recomputed from those same archives after correcting
daily boundaries: a midnight final mark closes the prior day and adds no extra day.
Historical data and raw runs are not copied into the runtime databases on i2.

The local 4h/15m datasets have no missing timestamps or duplicate rows. SOL has
51 zero-volume 15m candles; this prevents assuming continuous executable liquidity.
Six fixed 4h OHLCV samples (BTC/ETH/SOL on Jan 1, 2025 and Jan 1, 2026) were
compared with Binance's public klines endpoint. Sample checks do not authenticate
every row or eliminate survivorship bias in the three selected coins.

Next research must specify a distinct hypothesis or collect genuinely new data;
repeatedly tuning these same historical periods is not independent validation.
